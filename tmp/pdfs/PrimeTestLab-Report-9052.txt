        App Overview                                                              
      02                                                                          
                                                                                  
      Mhuri is a shared money app for families. After signing in and creating a family, members can record income and
      expenses, set budgets, save toward goals, track debts, keep a shared shopping list and chat with the household. A
      Family area holds members, currency and privacy options.                    
                                                                                  
                                                                                  
        Product Perspective                                                       
      03                                                                          
                                                                                  
      The app keeps everyday household money tasks in one calm, consistent place. The guided three-step setup, the
      over-budget warning and the "This exceeds the goal" confirmation show real care for the person using it. The
      biggest opportunities are making form messages consistent on every screen, fixing the dark theme contrast on the
      balance and plan cards, and correcting a few budget and currency labels that contradict each other.

        Summary                                                                   
      09
