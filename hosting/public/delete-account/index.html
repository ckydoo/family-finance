You can start deletion inside the app. Signing in and re-authenticating protects your family against unauthorized deletion.

Delete inside Mhuri

  1. Open Mhuri and sign in.
  2. Open Family and select Your Profile.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Confirm your password and follow the confirmation steps.

If you are a family member

You may choose Leave family. Your membership, profile association and sessions are removed. Past ledger entries may remain in the family’s accounting history under “Former Member,” without your name or photo.

If you own the family space

If other members remain, transfer ownership to another eligible adult first. If you are the sole owner, deleting the family space removes its budgets, transactions, goals, lists, requests, memberships and stored avatars through database cascade deletion.

Request deletion without app access

Email codzlabzim53@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject “Mhuri account deletion request.” We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request.

What is deleted or retained

  • Deleted: authentication identity, active sessions, profile details, push-notification tokens and memberships, subject to family ownership rules above.
  • Potentially retained: de-identified historical ledger attribution needed for the remaining family’s accounting and short-lived encrypted backups until their normal retention period expires.
