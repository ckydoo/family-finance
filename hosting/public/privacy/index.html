Mhuri is a family finance application for Android and iOS. We do not sell personal information or family financial records.

Information we process

  • Account information: email address, authentication identity and optional profile name/photo. Passwords are processed as cryptographic hashes by Supabase Auth; we cannot read them.
  • Family content: budgets, envelopes, transactions, goals, shopping lists, bills, recurring rules, family requests, roles and audit activity.
  • Child profiles: display name, avatar and permission settings. Children use a local profile PIN on a parent’s device; Mhuri does not require a child’s email address or phone number.
  • Technical information: an app-installation identifier, device push token, notification delivery/open events, screen views and privacy-limited usage events processed through Firebase.

How we use information

We use it to authenticate users, synchronize the household ledger, enforce family permissions, deliver important family notifications, maintain security, diagnose reliability and understand aggregate feature usage.

Information excluded from Analytics

We do not send transaction amounts, balances, transaction notes, family names, email addresses, profile photos or children’s profile data to Firebase Analytics. We do not use Analytics for cross-app advertising.

Service providers

  • Supabase: authentication, PostgreSQL database, file storage and server functions.
  • Google Firebase: push notification delivery and privacy-limited app analytics.
  • Google, Facebook or other sign-in providers: only when you choose the corresponding sign-in option.

Security and access

Network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Database row-level security restricts family records to authorized members of that family space. Device tokens and server credentials are protected separately and are not exposed in the mobile application.

Retention and deletion

Account and family data remain while the account or family space is active. Members who leave are detached from their identifying profile while historical ledger entries may remain attributed to “Former Member” to preserve household accounting. Encrypted operational backups expire according to the backup retention schedule. See the account deletion page for instructions.

Your choices

You can disable notifications in Mhuri Settings or in device settings, remove an optional avatar, leave a family, request an export, or delete your account. Device operating systems may provide additional Analytics and tracking controls.

Children

Mhuri’s child mode is controlled within a parent or guardian’s family account. We do not knowingly ask children for email addresses, phone numbers, advertising identifiers or independent payment credentials.
