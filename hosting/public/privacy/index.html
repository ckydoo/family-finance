Mhuri is a family finance application for Android and iOS. We do not sell personal information or family financial records.

Information we process

  • Account information: email address, authentication identity and optional profile name/photo. Passwords are securely hashed by our sign-in service; we cannot read them.
  • Family content: shared budgets, envelopes, transactions, goals, shopping lists, bills, recurring rules, family requests, roles, audit activity, task details and status, points, configured rewards, and reward redemption status.
  • Private account content: transactions and balance details in a member’s private account are restricted to that account’s owner and are not shared with the family.
  • Child profiles: display name, avatar and permission settings. Children use a local profile PIN on a parent’s device; Mhuri does not require a child’s email address or phone number.
  • Technical information: an app-installation identifier, device push token, notification delivery/open events, screen views and privacy-limited usage events processed through Firebase.

How we use information

We use it to authenticate users, synchronize the household ledger, enforce family permissions, deliver important family notifications, maintain security, diagnose reliability and understand aggregate feature usage.

Family tasks, points and rewards

Task assignments, completion and approval status, points, and reward redemption are shared with authorized members of the relevant family space. Points are an in-app tally and are not money or an account balance. Families define and provide any real-world rewards.

Information excluded from Analytics

We do not send transaction amounts, balances, transaction notes, family names, email addresses, profile photos or children’s profile data to Firebase Analytics. We do not use Analytics for cross-app advertising.

AI assistant

When an adult family member asks Ask Mhuri a question, the question, up to six recent chat turns, and a limited summary of relevant shared family financial records are sent to an external AI service to prepare a response. Private account records, raw notes, receipts and member profile fields are excluded; information typed into a question or chat may be included. The service processes this information under its privacy terms. Ask Mhuri chat history is saved locally on the device, scoped to the signed-in account and family space. It is not synced to other devices or shared with family members, and can be deleted from the chat history screen.

Service providers

Mhuri uses service providers for sign-in, data storage and synchronization, file storage, server functions, push notifications, app analytics, and AI responses. Google, Facebook or other sign-in providers receive information only when you choose the corresponding sign-in option.

Security and access

Network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Family records are restricted to authorized members of that family space. Device tokens and server credentials are protected separately and are not exposed in the mobile application.

Retention and deletion

Account and family data remain while the account or family space is active. Members who leave are detached from their identifying profile while historical ledger entries may remain attributed to “Former Member” to preserve household accounting. Encrypted operational backups expire according to the backup retention schedule. See the account deletion page for instructions.

Your choices

You can disable notifications in Mhuri Settings or in device settings, remove an optional avatar, leave a family, request an export, or delete your account. Device operating systems may provide additional Analytics and tracking controls.

Children

Mhuri’s child mode is controlled within a parent or guardian’s family account. We do not knowingly ask children for email addresses, phone numbers, advertising identifiers or independent payment credentials.
