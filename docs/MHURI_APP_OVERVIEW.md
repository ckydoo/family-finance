# Mhuri: The App at a Glance

## What Mhuri is

Mhuri is a shared money management app for families. It helps a household record income and spending, agree on budgets, keep track of savings and debts, and coordinate everyday shopping and family responsibilities. The goal is to make family finances easier to see and discuss together.

Mhuri is designed around a family space. One person can create a space and invite relatives, or join a space using an invitation. Members can have different roles and permissions. Each family chooses its main currency and can set up spending categories that match its household. The app also supports a second currency.

## How someone gets started

1. **Create an account or accept an invitation.** A new user can start a family space or join one that already exists.
2. **Set up the household.** Choose the family name, currency, budget cycle and payday, then pick starter spending categories such as groceries, rent, transport or school. Categories can be customized.
3. **Add family members.** Invite adults and children with the appropriate roles. What each person can do depends on their role and permissions.
4. **Build a useful starting picture.** Add expected income, set category budgets, and enter any current savings goals or debts. The Home screen guides a new household through its first steps.

## The main app, from the bottom navigation

The adult experience has four main areas and a central **+** button for recording a transaction.

### Home: see the household at a glance

Home brings together the family’s current money picture and useful shortcuts. It helps members see how the budget is progressing, review recent activity, and get to family tools. Depending on setup and activity, it can show prompts or reminders that help the household keep its information up to date.

### Budgets: decide where the money should go

Budgets organize spending into household categories, sometimes called envelopes. A family sets limits for a budget period and compares recorded spending with those limits. Members can review income, allocations and available amounts, adjust budgets, and reuse the previous period’s setup. Basic recurring payment rules help account for regular expenses.

### Savings: track goals and obligations

Savings brings together savings goals and debts or money owed. Families can set a target, record contributions, track progress, and record debt repayments. The app also supports shared savings arrangements such as Mukando. Optional planning tools can model savings contributions and debt payoff scenarios.

### Lists: coordinate household shopping

The shared shopping list lets family members add items, track what still needs to be bought, and record items being purchased or completed. Shopping estimates can be compared with the household budget. Completed purchases can be checked out and used to keep the family’s spending records useful.

### The central + button: record money activity

From the main adult navigation, the **+** button opens a quick form for income or an expense. A transaction can include an amount, date, category, payment method, notes, and, when useful, a receipt or a split across categories. Recording transactions is how the family keeps budgets and reports grounded in what actually happened.

## Other family tools

These tools are available from Home, the family area, or related screens rather than as separate bottom-navigation tabs:

- **Activity and reports:** Review recorded income and spending over a period, inspect category breakdowns, and export transaction data as CSV. Additional reports and a financial health review provide deeper analysis.
- **Bills and commitments:** Track planned one-time or recurring obligations, due dates, reminders, and partial payments. This gives the family a place to monitor upcoming costs alongside the budget.
- **Payday planning:** Look ahead from current balances and expected income to estimate upcoming cash flow, spot possible shortfalls, and explore what-if scenarios.
- **Family chat:** Discuss household finances and share context with the family. Chat can include references to budgets, shopping items, and other app information. Polls, pinned messages, and advanced search are additional collaboration tools.
- **Family tasks, points and rewards:** Family admins create and assign responsibilities, set due dates and point values, and review submitted work. Approved points are attributed to the member who completed the task. Admins can configure family-defined rewards, which eligible members can redeem when they have enough points. Points are an in-app tally, not money or a balance.
- **Settings and family management:** Manage profile and family members, invitations, permissions, preferences, synchronization, and subscription information.

## How a typical family uses Mhuri

1. At the start of a budget period, the family checks income and sets amounts for major categories such as food, housing, transport and school.
2. As money arrives or is spent, a member records it. The budget view updates so the family can see how actual spending compares with the plan.
3. Members add planned purchases to the shared list and coordinate who will buy them. The household can use checkout and transaction records to keep spending aligned.
4. The family tracks goals, debts and regular obligations, then checks reports or upcoming cash flow when deciding what to do next.
5. Family members can discuss choices in chat and review the same shared information, subject to their roles.

## Free use and Mhuri Plus

Mhuri’s everyday foundation is free: family spaces and collaboration, income and expense records, budgets, savings goals, debts and repayments, shopping lists, family tasks, chat, basic recurring bills, and standard CSV reporting.

**Mhuri Plus** is the optional paid subscription for more advanced planning and review. The current Plus design includes smart bills and commitments, payday cash-flow planning, period preparation, financial health reviews, advanced savings and debt planning, and selected chat tools. One subscription is intended to cover the family space, so its members can use the included features together.

The product’s clearest promise is helping a family understand its day-to-day money and plan what is coming next. Plus extends that with deeper forward-looking tools; it is not required to record ordinary income, expenses or budgets.

## In one sentence

**Mhuri helps a family manage everyday money together, keep household plans visible, and prepare for upcoming financial needs.**
